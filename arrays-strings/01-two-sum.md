## Problem: Two Sum (Easy)

**Link:** https://leetcode.com/problems/two-sum/

### Approach

We use two nested loops to check every pair of elements in the array.
If the sum of two elements equals the target, their indices are returned.

### Complexity

- Time: O(n²)
- Space: O(1)
